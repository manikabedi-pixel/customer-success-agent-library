Overview

The Customer Onboarding Coordination Agent is the third component of a 12-agent Customer Success AI Agent Library.

It helps Customer Success and Implementation teams assess onboarding coordination, identify planning gaps, and organize issues requiring attention.

Agent #3 consumes the structured onboarding plan produced by Agent #2.

It evaluates the available planning and progress information without assuming that proposed activities have been completed.

The agent does not autonomously execute onboarding tasks or approve onboarding activities.

Business Problem

Customer onboarding often involves multiple stakeholders, milestones, and dependencies.

Even when an onboarding plan exists, teams still need to coordinate:

* Task ownership.
* Due dates.
* Milestone progress.
* Dependencies.
* Blocked activities.
* Missing information.
* Approval requirements.

Manual coordination can become difficult when information is distributed across multiple systems or teams.

Agent #3 helps organize the available information into a structured coordination assessment.

Core Capabilities

* Loads structured onboarding plans from Agent #2.
* Evaluates task ownership and scheduling completeness.
* Identifies missing owners and due dates.
* Reviews milestone and task progress information.
* Distinguishes reported completion from verified completion.
* Identifies coordination issues.
* Generates proposed recommendations.
* Assesses whether onboarding coordination can proceed.
* Produces structured coordination reports.
* Preserves human approval and execution safeguards.

Output

Agent #3 produces a structured coordination report.

Example:

outputs/onboarding_coordinator/coordination_report_<deal_id>_report.json

The report may include:

* Customer and deal information.
* Source plan status.
* Coordination readiness.
* Task and milestone progress.
* Missing owners.
* Missing due dates.
* Coordination issues.
* Recommendations.
* Approval information.
* Human-review requirements.
* Execution authorization status.

Example Development Scenario

The synthetic Northstar Software onboarding plan was used to evaluate the coordination workflow.

The scenario contained eight proposed tasks.

Human Review and Safety

Agent #3 separates coordination assessment from actual execution.

It does not automatically:

* Assign real task owners.
* Commit delivery dates.
* Execute onboarding tasks.
* Confirm task completion without evidence.
* Approve an onboarding plan.
* Authorize external onboarding activities.

Recommendations remain subject to human review.

Current Limitations

* The quality of the coordination assessment depends on the source onboarding plan.
* Missing owners and dates require external confirmation.
* Reported progress may not represent verified completion.
* The agent does not independently validate customer-side activities.
* The prototype does not replace a project management platform or human implementation coordination.
